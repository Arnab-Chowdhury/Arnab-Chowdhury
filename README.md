## Research & collaboration

My interests include **traffic operations, public transport, and transport network optimisation**, with an emphasis on problems relevant to cities such as Dhaka.

### Featured project: traffic signal optimisation in SUMO

[**Evolutionary game-based signal timing — Bijoy Sarani, Dhaka**](https://github.com/Arnab-Chowdhury/sumo-thesis)

This project uses evolutionary game theory to allocate signal green time in a SUMO model of Bijoy Sarani intersection. It includes heterogeneous traffic calibration, survey-based demand, and comparisons with fixed-time signal plans.

- **Tools and methods:** Python, SUMO/TraCI, evolutionary game theory, and simulation-based evaluation.
- **In the repository:** network and demand files, signal-control code, experiment scripts, and documented results.

## Astronomy & outreach

I volunteer with the **Bangladesh Olympiad on Astronomy and Astrophysics (BDOAA)** as an advisor and have served as a Bangladesh team leader at the **International Olympiad on Astronomy and Astrophysics (IOAA)**.

Teaching, developing learning materials, and helping students work through unfamiliar problems are a big part of what I enjoy.
